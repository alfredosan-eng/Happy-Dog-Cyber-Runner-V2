# Game Design — Happy Dog Cyber Runner V2

## Progression
- Level 1 starts at 0 bones.
- Every 20 collected bones advances the level.
- Speed increases gently per level.

## LIFE
- Starts at 3 lives.
- Maximum is 10 lives.
- 🩷 Extra Life power-up restores one life.

## Power-ups
- 🛡 Shield — blocks one collision.
- 🧲 Magnet — attracts nearby bones.
- 🩷 Extra Life — adds one life up to the 10-life maximum.

## Environment
The city uses moving low/high building layers for parallax depth. Aircraft occasionally cross the sky at a slower speed than the city.

## Traffic
Cars, taxis, buses, motorcycles and street sweepers.
